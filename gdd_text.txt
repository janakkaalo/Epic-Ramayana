Comprehensive Game Design and 
Technical Specification: Ramayana 3D 
Action-RPG 
The translation of the ancient Sanskrit epic, the Ramayana, into a fully realized, 
three-dimensional interactive digital environment represents a monumental challenge in both 
narrative architecture and computational execution. Comprising over twenty-four thousand 
verses divided into seven distinct Kandas or books, the epic serves as a foundational pillar of 
global philosophy and theology, presenting an unparalleled canvas for a 3D Action-Role Playing 
Game (RPG).1 To execute this project autonomously using the capabilities of modern 
developmental frameworks like Replit Agent v4, the artificial intelligence requires highly 
structured, deterministic parameters to generate the full chronological story, culturally 
authentic mechanics, and sophisticated WebGL graphics.3 The developmental infrastructure 
must rely on a Three.js rendering framework and the Cannon-es physics engine to handle the 
complex celestial warfare and environmental puzzles inherent in the source material.3 This 
document serves as the master architectural blueprint, providing the technical logic and 
narrative sequence required to scaffold, compile, and refine the complete application—from 
the foundational Vite bundler and Node.js backend to the final post-processing rendering 
pass.3 
Narrative Architecture and State Machine Flow 
The sprawling timeline of the Ramayana, spanning from the miraculous birth of the princes in 
Ayodhya to the cataclysmic battle at Lanka and the eventual establishment of Rama Rajya, 
cannot be effectively translated into an interactive experience without compartmentalizing the 
narrative into discrete, executable game states and playable levels.1 The narrative architecture 
must follow a framing device, opening with the sage Valmiki inquiring of the celestial 
messenger Narada regarding the existence of a perfect human being.4 This device establishes 
the game's central philosophical theme: the concept of Maryada Purushottam, or the Supreme 
Person of Boundaries.5 The developmental agent must structure the game into five distinct 
acts, tracking player progress, moral alignment (Dharma), and inventory states through a 
centralized, highly secure state machine.3 
Act I: The Incarnation and the Training of the Princes 
The first act functions as the prologue and mechanical tutorial, establishing the historical and 
moral foundations of the Ikshvaku dynasty and introducing the core spiritual mechanics of the 
game engine.1 The engine must initialize the state machine in approximately 12241 BCE, 
centering on the capital city of Ayodhya.7 The environment must reflect the architectural 
grandeur of the Kosala Kingdom, with intricate stone masonry and wide chariot-lanes rendered 
using high-fidelity WebGL shaders.8 Following the cinematic representation of the 
Putrakameshti Yajna and the subsequent birth of Rama, Bharata, Lakshmana, and Shatrughna, 
the gameplay transitions into an instructional phase.1 
The player assumes control of a sixteen-year-old Rama, tasked with accompanying the sage 
Vishwamitra to protect his forest hermitage from demonic disruptions.1 This level introduces 
the primary combat system: precision archery and the invocation of divine Astras.10 The first 
major encounter against the demoness Tataka serves as a combat tutorial for targeting vital 
points (Marmas).1 Successful completion of this quest unlocks the initial tier of the "AstraVidya" 
framework, granting the player access to supernatural projectiles.3 The act culminates in the 
kingdom of Mithila, where the player must solve a physics-based puzzle involving the weight 
and tension of the celestial Bow of Shiva.1 The engine must utilize the Cannon.js library to 
calculate the structural stress on the bow mesh, resulting in its physical breakage once the 
player successfully applies sufficient force through an interactive timing minigame.1 
Act II: The Forest Exile and the Crisis of Dharma 
The second act transitions from the prosperity of Ayodhya into a high-stakes survival and 
stealth environment, focusing on the political intrigue and subsequent fourteen-year exile.2 The 
timeline shifts to approximately 12223 BCE, where the player must navigate complex dialogue 
trees against the manipulated Queen Kaikeyi and the heartbroken King Dasharatha.7 This 
chapter introduces the "Maryada" system, where the player's adherence to "Pitru Dharma" (filial 
duty) influences the world-state.4 Choosing to accept the exile without resistance, despite the 
option for a military revolt, permanently bolsters the player's "Dharma" score, which in turn 
enhances the efficacy of future divine interventions.15 
The gameplay then shifts to the Dandaka forest, requiring the player to manage environmental 
resources and establish a hermitage for Sita and Lakshmana.1 This environment features dense 
procedural generation of Ashoka and Shala trees, utilizing Three.js's InstancedMesh to maintain 
performance.3 The primary threat comes from the demoness Surpanakha, whose attempts at 
seduction and subsequent mutilation trigger a series of boss encounters against the generals 
Khara and Dushana.1 The act reaches its narrative climax with the abduction of Sita by Ravana, 
utilizing a "Maya" (illusion) mechanic where the demon Maricha assumes the form of a golden 
deer to lure the player away.1 This sequence forces the player to manage Lakshmana's 
AI-controlled defensive stance while chasing the high-speed, specularly-shaded deer mesh.1 
Act III: The Kishkindha Alliance and the Vanara Scout 
The third act dramatically expands the gameplay scope, introducing faction-based strategy 
and high-mobility exploration in the southern subcontinent.7 The player travels to the 
boulder-strewn landscape of Kishkindha, modeled after the unique topography of the Hampi 
region.20 This level focuses on the conflict between the brothers Vali and Sugriva. The engine 
must support a critical moral decision point: the player, as Rama, must decide whether to 
engage the invincible Vali in open combat or intervene from the shadows.21 Choosing the latter 
fulfills the "Aapad Dharma" (duty in adversity) logic, securing the alliance with the Vanara army 
but creating a permanent "Karmic Weight" in the backend database.3 
Once the alliance is established, the perspective shifts to Hanuman for the "Sundara" level.1 This 
segment introduces the "Ashta Siddhi" mechanics, where the player can dynamically toggle 
Hanuman’s size and mass to navigate the vertical cliffs of the Rishyamukha mountain.24 The act 
culminates in Hanuman’s colossal leap across the ocean mesh to Lanka. The physics engine 
must calculate momentum and air resistance, while the rendering pipeline uses custom GLSL 
shaders to simulate the crystalline "Vaidurya" waters of the Pampa Lake.17 
Act IV: The Infiltration of Lanka and the Divine Message 
The fourth act focuses on social stealth, intelligence gathering, and psychological warfare 
within the enemy capital.27 Controlling Hanuman, the player must infiltrate the "Golden City" of 
Lanka, rendered with advanced screen-space reflection (SSR) shaders to represent its metallic 
architecture.1 The primary objective is to locate Sita in the Ashoka Vatika without triggering an 
all-out alarm from the Rakshasa guards.1 This level utilizes "Anima" (size-shrinking) to slip 
through fortifications and "Vasitva" (influence) to mesmerize minor enemies.23 
Following the successful delivery of Rama’s message, the gameplay shifts into an aggressive 
"Lanka Dahan" (Burning of Lanka) phase.27 The player utilizes fire-based area-of-effect (AoE) 
attacks to degrade the city’s environmental mesh, testing the engine's particle system and 
dynamic deformation capabilities.17 The act concludes with Hanuman’s return to the mainland 
and the subsequent engineering challenge: the construction of the "Nala Setu" bridge. The 
player must coordinate the Vanara Sena to place "zero-gravity" stones on the water mesh, 
requiring a complex physics-mesh interaction to simulate a floating structure capable of 
supporting an army.7 
Act V: The Great War and the Fall of the Demon King 
The fifth and final act encompasses the cataclysmic eighteen-day Battle of Lanka, transitioning 
from standard RPG exploration into large-scale 3D battlefield management.7 The Yuka AI library 
must be fully optimized to handle thousands of concurrent entities, including the Vanara 
infantry and the Rakshasa legions.3 The player alternates between controlling Rama in precision 
duels and commanding the army in a hybrid RTS interface.3 
The battles are structured as a series of high-difficulty boss encounters against Ravana’s family 
and generals.30 To defeat Indrajit, the player must utilize Lakshmana’s "Twelve-Year 
Sleeplessness" trait to see through Indrajit’s "Maya" invisibility shaders.35 The encounter with the 
giant Kumbhakarna requires the player to coordinate a "Gulliver-style" containment strategy, 
utilizing the Vanara Sena to weigh down the demon’s limbs before delivering a lethal strike.38 
The war culminates in the final duel between Rama and Ravana. The player must execute a 
"Ten-Head Decapitation" mechanic, where severed heads regenerate in real-time unless the 
player targets Ravana’s navel during a specific vulnerability window.18 The application concludes 
with the "Agni Pariksha" (Trial by Fire), the return to Ayodhya, and a free-roam epilogue where 
the player can explore the perfect geometric utopian state of Rama Rajya.2

The character design and underlying statistical architecture must reflect the absolute and 
almost incomprehensible power scaling described in the Sanskrit texts.3 Traditional RPG 
leveling is insufficient; instead, the developmental agent must hard-code these parameters into 
an Entity Component System (ECS), ensuring that collision detection and damage output scale 
exponentially based on a character's designated theological tier.3

The Maryada and Dharma Alignment Engine 
To accurately represent the theological depth of the Ramayana, standard RPG alignment 
systems like "Good versus Evil" are entirely discarded.3 In their place, the engine must construct 
a sophisticated Maryada and Dharma Tracking Engine.4 This system monitors the player's 
adherence to cosmic duty and moral limits, compounding these variables to influence both 
narrative branches and statistical outcomes.3 
The Maryada System (Limits of the Universe) 
The concept of Maryada refers to the boundaries of the universe and the limits of appropriate 
behavior for a given role.5 The engine tracks the player across four primary role-based metrics: 
●​ Pati Dharma (Husband’s Duty): Evaluates the player's choices regarding the protection 
and respect of Sita.15 
●​ Pitru Dharma (Son’s Duty): Measures adherence to the instructions and honor of King 
Dasharatha and the three mothers.4 
●​ Bhratru Dharma (Brother’s Duty): Tracks the loyalty and support provided to Lakshmana, 
Bharata, and Shatrughna.13 
●​ Raj Dharma (King’s Duty): Influences the "Rama Rajya" world-state, prioritizing the 
welfare of the citizens over personal desire.13 
Dharma Points and Karmic Weight 
High Dharma scores, earned through truthfulness (Satya) and self-control (Atman), unlock the 
highest-tier Astras and increase the radius of protective auras like the "Laxman Rekha".4 
Conversely, the engine utilizes a delayed-consequence logic for Karma. A choice made early in 
the game—such as the intervention in the Bali-Sugriva duel—is mathematically weighted to 
generate specialized "Nemesis" enemies in the later acts.3 The engine tracks these variables in a 
backend database (SQLite), which directly governs the sorting algorithms for the game's 
multiple endings.3 
The AstraVidya Framework and Divine Weapons 
Standard melee combat is supplemented by Astras, celestial weapons invoked through 
mantras that represent the game's sophisticated magic system.3 The developmental agent 
must implement this through the "AstraVidya" framework—a tag-based architecture that uses 
JSON configuration files to define the properties of each weapon.3 
Mechanical Implementation of Astras 
The framework relies on a hexagonal combination interface where players can chain elemental 
properties.3 The engine must program strict tactical counters: if an enemy invokes the fiery 
Agneyastra, the player must counter with the water-based Varunastra.10

The Ashta Siddhi Mastery (Hanuman Mechanics) 
For the playable Hanuman, standard RPG skills are replaced by the eight mystical perfections.23 
●​ Anima (Shrinking): Used for stealth and infiltration puzzles in Lanka.24 
●​ Mahima (Expanding): Essential for high-altitude combat and lifting environmental objects 
like the Dronagiri mountain.24 
●​ Garima (Heaviness): Prevents knockback; allows Hanuman to become an immovable 
object during a swarm.23 
●​ Laghima (Weightlessness): Enables high-speed flight and traversal over the sea mesh.24 
●​ Prapti (Attainment): Allows the player to instantaneously acquire resources or medicine 
(Sanjeevani) from any distance.23 
●​ Prakamya (Will): Grants adaptive resilience, including underwater locomotion and 
temporary invulnerability.23 
●​ Isitva (Nature Control): Commands the fire during the Lanka Dahan sequence.23 
●​ Vasitva (Mesmerism): Tames wild animals and manipulates the mind-states of low-tier 
Rakshasas.25 
Environmental Art Direction and Level Design 
The visual language must accurately reflect the grandeur of ancient India, drawing inspiration 
from Vedic, Mauryan, and Vijayanagar architectural precedents.3 The developmental agent 
must utilize Level of Detail (LOD) swapping and aggressive instanced rendering to maintain 
performance across these diverse hub worlds.3 
Ayodhya and the Kosala Kingdom 
The capital city acts as the primary hub for Act I and the Epilogue.1 
●​ Architecture: Imposing stone masonry, wide streets for chariots, and parks laid out in a 
"Karmuka" (bow) shape.9 
●​ Visual Centerpiece: The Ram Mandir, featuring meticulously reconstructed 3D layers of 
white MDF-style textures and golden-lit interiors.49 
●​ Atmosphere: High-luminosity lighting with a "Rama Rajya" post-processing filter to convey 
a utopian state of peace and order.2 
The Kishkindha Region (Hampi Landscape) 
The kingdom of the Vanaras is characterized by a hilly, boulder-filled terrain.20 
●​ Environment: Massive granite boulders strewn across the landscape, perfect for 
defensive hideouts and high-mobility platforming.20 
●​ Pampa Sarovar: A pristine lake rendered with crystalline, pellucid water meshes that 
mimic the specular highlights of cat's-eye gems.21 
●​ Level Design: Caves and ravines (Rishyamook) that serve as hubs for the Vanara leaders, 
decorated with primitive yet sacred stone idols.20 
The Golden City of Lanka 
Ravana’s citadel is situated on the three peaks of Mount Trikuta.29 
●​ Materiality: Constructed entirely of gold. The rendering pipeline must apply custom GLSL 
shaders to all architectural meshes to handle complex reflections.1 
●​ Verticality: High-altitude fortifications and the Ashoka Vatika grove, featuring tropical 
Ashoka trees and Shiva-dedicated statuary.1 
●​ Key Asset: The Pushpaka Vimana, a vast aerial chariot rendered as a mobile flying palace 
that can be boarded during specific Act V sequences.1 
Technical Implementation via Replit Agent v4 
The execution of this comprehensive design document relies entirely on the autonomous 
coding capabilities of the developmental agent, interfaced with Three.js and the Yuka AI 
engine.3 The initialization phase requires establishing a Node.js backend and a Vite bundler to 
manage high-speed module loading.3 
ECS and Performance Optimization 
To manage the millions of monkeys and demons engaged in the Battle of Lanka, the agent must 
utilize InstancedMesh for rendering.3 This reduces draw calls and prevents the browser 
environment from crashing under the volume of entities.3 The AI pathfinding for the Vanara 
Sena must be offloaded to WebWorkers to ensure the main UI thread remains responsive at 
60fps.3 
Physics and Interaction Logic 
Integration with the Cannon-es physics engine is paramount for the AstraVidya and Ashta 
Siddhi frameworks.3 
●​ Momentum Calculation: Used for Hanuman's leap and the high-speed impact of chariot 
charges.3 
●​ Collision Decoupling: In "Maya Sabha" or illusionary sequences, the engine must decouple 
visual meshes from their collision bodies, forcing the player to rely on "Discernment" 
abilities to find the correct path.3 
●​ Dynamic Deformation: Used for the earthquake events during the construction of the 
Nala Setu and the falling of mountain-sized bosses like Kumbhakarna.7 
AI Behavior Trees 
The Yuka AI library manages the complex behaviors of both the Apostolic-style Vanara 
companions and the diverse Rakshasa hordes.3 
●​ Indrajit AI: Programmed with a sophisticated FSM that monitors the player's detection 
state. If the player is blinded by a dark energy arrow, the AI enters a "Silent Strike" mode.30 
●​ Ravana AI: Tracks ten independent health pools for the heads. The AI chooses its active 
head based on the player’s current combat style—using a "Warrior" head for melee and a 
"Scholar" head to counter Astras.40 
By feeding this exhaustive architectural, narrative, and systemic blueprint into the 
developmental agent, the developer ensures the accurate, robust, and technologically 
advanced realization of the Ramayana as a definitive, story-driven interactive experience.3
